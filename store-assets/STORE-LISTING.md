## English

### App name (30 characters max)

```
What's My Pick!
```

### Short description (80 characters max)

```
Guess what others picked, before they guess yours. A party game for 2-12.
```

*(72 characters)*

### Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Everyone secretly picks a word. Then it is up to you: ask sharp yes/no questions, listen closely to how everyone answers, and guess their word before they guess yours.

What's My Pick! is a fast, social guessing game for 2 to 12 players. Great on the sofa, in the classroom, at a party, or online with friends anywhere.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Create a game and share your room code, or drop into an open lobby
• Agree on a category, then secretly pick your word
• Take turns asking the whole group one yes/no question
• Everyone answers about their own secret word
• Collect the clues in your notebook and strike at the right moment
• Every correct guess scores a point. The sharpest thinker wins

WHY IT IS FUN
• Real-time: every question and answer lands instantly for everyone
• Hint rounds where each player gives something away, right when it gets tense
• A notebook that tracks what you know about every single opponent
• Chat with emotes to cheer, bluff and wind each other up
• No account needed. Pick a name and play

PRACTISE ON YOUR OWN
Nobody online? Play a full match offline against bots that answer honestly about their own word. The easiest way to learn the tactics before you take on friends.

COLLECT AND SHOW OFF
Earn Picks and levels as you play, and unlock over a hundred emblems, banners, borders, titles and name colours, from Common all the way to Mythic. Daily rewards, challenges and a win streak keep every session worth playing.

CATEGORIES
Free: Pets, Actors, Movies, Football Players, European Countries and Household Objects. Want more? Optional packs add dozens of extra categories, from pop culture to fantasy and food.

Fully playable in English and Dutch.

Have fun, and please tell us what you think.
```

## Notes

- **Character counts** are safely inside Play's limits (80 for short, 4000 for
  full). If you reuse this on the App Store, note it uses a 30-character name,
  a 30-character subtitle and a 170-character promotional text instead, so the
  short description needs trimming there.
- **Do not add** phrases like "best game" or "number 1", and do not list
  competitor names. Play rejects listings for both.
- **Category**: Games → Word (Trivia also fits). Tags: multiplayer, party,
  word, puzzle.
- **Contact details**: use the same support URL and email as the App Store
  listing so both stores point at one place.
- The **bots** wording is deliberate. They answer from a hand-authored fact
  table, so describing them as answering "honestly" is accurate.
