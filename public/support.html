Support — What's My Pick!

The party guessing game for 2–12 players. Answers to the most common questions below.

How do I play?

Create a lobby (public or private) and share the room code, or join a friend's code. The host picks a category, everyone chooses a secret word, then players take turns asking yes/no questions. Every answer is saved in your Notebook — use the clues to guess everyone's word. Correct guesses score points; most points wins! The full guide is inside the app under the ? button.

My friends can't join my game

  • Double-check the 6-letter room code — it must match exactly.
  • The game must still be in the lobby (or word-entry) phase; new players can't join mid-round.
  • Rooms hold a maximum of 12 players.
  • Everyone needs an internet connection — the game is played live.

I bought a category pack but don't have it

Open Settings → Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so this also brings packs back after reinstalling or switching phones.

What are Picks? Do I have to pay?

Picks are the in-game currency, earned only by playing — daily rewards, matches, and challenges. They unlock cosmetics (emblems, banners, borders, titles, name styles). Cosmetics are never sold for real money and nothing is pay-to-win. The only real-money purchases are optional category packs.

Will I lose my progress?

No — your Picks, cosmetics, and stats are automatically backed up to your iCloud account, so they survive reinstalls and follow you to a new iPhone. You can check the backup status in Settings ("Progress backed up to iCloud"). Purchased packs are tied to your Apple ID and can always be restored via Settings → Restore Purchases.

Someone is behaving badly

Tap any player's card to Mute them (hides their chat messages for you) or Report player. Hosts can also remove players from the lobby. Names, chat, and custom words are automatically filtered for offensive language.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete Profile permanently removes your profile and game records. See the Privacy Policy for details on what is stored.
