Privacy Policy — What's My Pick!

Last updated: August 2026

What we store

  • Display name — the nickname you choose. It is visible to other players you play with.
  • Gameplay data — rooms, questions, answers, guesses, chat messages, scores, and your cosmetic progression (level, Picks, unlocked items). Stored to make multiplayer work.
  • Optional account — if you sign in (email, Apple, or Google), your login identifier is stored by our backend provider (Supabase) to let you keep your account across devices.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No sale or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Category packs are one-time purchases processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. All other items are earned by playing and are purely cosmetic.

User content & safety

Names, chat messages, and custom words are filtered for offensive language. You can report any player from their player card; reports are reviewed and repeat offenders are removed. Hosts can remove players from their lobby.

Data deletion

You can delete your profile and game records at any time from Settings → Delete Profile. This permanently removes your game records, chat messages, progression profile, and local identity (including the iCloud backup), and cannot be undone. Chat messages in rooms that already expired are deleted automatically by our cleanup job. Category pack purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not your profile, and can always be restored via Settings → Restore Purchases.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and collects the minimum data needed to play.
